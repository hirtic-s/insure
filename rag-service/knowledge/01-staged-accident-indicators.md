# Staged accident indicators

Staged or induced collisions share a recognisable pattern. No single indicator proves fraud; treat several together as grounds for escalation.

- Low-speed collision with disproportionately large injury claims, especially soft-tissue injuries that cannot be objectively verified.
- No independent witness, or witnesses who are connected to the claimant or to each other.
- Accident on a quiet local road or in a parking lot with no camera coverage.
- Claimant reports the incident late, or only after the policy was recently started or its cover increased.
- Vehicle damage inconsistent with the described impact, or damage that predates the reported incident.
