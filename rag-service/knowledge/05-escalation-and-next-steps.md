# Escalation and recommended next steps

Recommended investigator workflow once a claim is flagged:

1. Confirm the facts: policy in force on the incident date, policy start date relative to the incident, identity of claimant and driver.
2. Collect independent evidence: police report, scene photographs, repair estimates from a second shop, medical records.
3. Take a recorded statement and note inconsistencies against the written submission.
4. Check for prior claims by the same claimant, address, phone number or provider.
5. If two or more strong indicators remain unresolved, escalate to the Special Investigations Unit (SIU) before payment and record the reasoning.
