# Claim amount and injury ratios

Inflated claims often show an injury component that is large relative to the total.

- Compare the injury claim to the total claim. An injury share above roughly half of a low-value vehicle claim warrants medical record review.
- Compare the claimed total with the vehicle's market value; a repair estimate approaching or exceeding value is a flag.
- Round-number amounts and amounts just under internal approval thresholds deserve a second look.
- Request itemised invoices and treatment records. Ask the provider to confirm dates of treatment independently.
