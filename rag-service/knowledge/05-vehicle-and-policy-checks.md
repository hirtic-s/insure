# Vehicle and policy checks

## Vehicle value
Compare the claimed repair or loss with the vehicle's value and age. A loss close to the value of an older vehicle deserves an independent valuation.

## Sport and high-value vehicles
A sport or high-value vehicle with a collision or all-perils policy has more to claim than a liability-only policy. The model learned this pattern, so a high score there is driven by the policy more than by the incident.

## Agent type
Check whether the policy was sold through an external or an internal agent. Look for patterns across other claims from the same agent only when several claims already point the same way. One claim is not a pattern.

## Deductible and cover
Confirm the deductible and what the policy covers. A liability policy does not pay for damage to the policyholder's own vehicle, so a claim for that damage should be checked against the policy wording.
