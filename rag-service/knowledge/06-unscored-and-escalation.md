# Unscored claims and escalation

## Unscored is not low risk
An unscored claim could not be scored because the scoring service was unavailable. It is retried automatically. Until it is scored, review it manually and do not treat it as low risk.

## Order of work
Open claims in score order, and read the factors and the hand-check list before the claim itself. Spend the first pass confirming or clearing the specific points raised, not looking for fraud in general.

## Escalation
Refer a claim to the special investigations unit when documents contradict each other, when independent evidence contradicts the account, or when several hand-check points hold and the claimant cannot explain them. Record what was checked and what was found at each step.

## Fair treatment
Decisions rest on documented evidence from the investigation. A score, a factor or a hand-check point is a reason to look, never a reason to refuse payment.
