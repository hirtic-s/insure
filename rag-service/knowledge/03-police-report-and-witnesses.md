# Police report and witnesses

## No police report
A missing police report is common in ordinary claims, so it is a prompt to ask, not a finding. Ask when and why no report was filed, whether the loss was reported to anyone else, and whether a report can still be obtained.

## No witness
A claim with no witness has nothing independent to corroborate the account. Look for other independent evidence: photographs, dash-camera footage, nearby cameras, repair-shop records and the other party's account.

## When both are missing
No report and no witness together leave the claimant's own account as the only evidence. That justifies a closer look at the documents, and nothing more on its own.
