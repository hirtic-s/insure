# Claim history and timing

## Past claims
Two or more past claims are a reason to read the earlier claims and look for repeated circumstances, repeated repairers or repeated parties. A count alone says nothing about this claim.

## Accident soon after the policy started
An accident within a week of the policy starting is worth checking. Confirm when cover began, whether the vehicle was already damaged when the policy was bought, and whether the policy was changed recently.

## Address change before the claim
A recent address change can explain why contact details or garaging details differ from the policy. Confirm the garaging address and check whether it matches the accident area.

## Delay between accident and claim
A long gap between the accident and the claim should be asked about. Many claimants have ordinary reasons, such as waiting for a repair estimate or recovering from an injury.
