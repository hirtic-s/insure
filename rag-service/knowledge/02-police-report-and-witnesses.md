# Police reports and witness corroboration

A police report filed at the scene is the strongest independent corroboration of a loss. Absence of a report is not proof of fraud, but it removes the main external check.

- Request the report number and verify it directly with the issuing agency; do not rely on a copy supplied by the claimant.
- Where a witness is recorded, contact them separately and compare their account with the claimant's on time, direction of travel and number of occupants.
- A claim with no police report and no witness should be prioritised for a recorded statement before any payment.
